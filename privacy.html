Privacy Policy

Nexo Finds Publisher — Last updated: 7 November 2026

Nexo Finds operates Nexo Finds Publisher, a private tool for managing original content for the Nexo Finds Pinterest business account. This notice explains how the Publisher handles data.

Account connection and data used

The account owner authorizes the connection on Pinterest through OAuth. The Publisher receives authorization tokens, their expiry information and the permissions granted. It uses these to verify the authorized account, look up destination boards and verify the results of publication attempts. Pinterest handles the account sign-in and consent screens.

Editorial workflow and publication

The Publisher records original images, draft titles and descriptions, optional destination links, approval records, and scheduled publication times. Each Pin requires individual approval by the account owner before it can be scheduled for submission to Pinterest. The Publisher records delivery outcomes, including the Pin identifier returned for a successful submission or an error code when a request fails.

Storage and access

Authorization tokens are encrypted in server-side storage. Access to the Publisher is restricted to authorized operators. A short-lived cookie helps verify that the Pinterest authorization response belongs to the browser that started the connection.

Service providers

The Publisher uses Pinterest's API for authorized account and publishing operations. Its hosting and storage providers process the records needed to run the service. Pinterest describes its own data practices in its Privacy Policy.

Access, revocation and deletion

The account owner can revoke authorization from their Pinterest account settings.
