Privacy Policy

Nexo Finds Publisher — Last updated: 10 October 2026

Nexo Finds Publisher is a private, internal application being developed to manage original Pinterest content for the Nexo Finds account. It is not available for public sign-up or use by third parties.

Data accessed

If and when the Pinterest integration is enabled, the application is intended to access only data necessary to manage the authorized Nexo Finds Pinterest account, such as account identifiers, boards, Pins, publishing metadata and authorization tokens. The exact permissions will be limited to those granted during Pinterest authorization.

Purpose and legal basis

The intended purpose is to organize, schedule, publish and verify Pins individually approved by the account owner.

The application will access the Pinterest account only after authorization by the account owner. Any processing of personal data will be limited to what is necessary for the internal publishing workflow and handled in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Storage and security

The application is not yet operational. Before activation, Nexo Finds will implement appropriate security measures, including restricted access to authorization credentials and secure storage of operational records.

Authentication tokens and other confidential credentials will not be exposed through the public website or repository. Data will not be sold.

Sharing

When enabled, the integration will communicate with Pinterest to perform authorized actions. Nexo Finds does not intend to provide account data to advertisers or unrelated third parties through this application.

Retention and deletion

Operational data will be retained only for as long as necessary for the authorized publishing workflow.

The account owner may revoke application access through Pinterest settings and request deletion of any personal data held by Nexo Finds by contacting nexoyt2026gpt@gmail.com , subject to applicable legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning Nexo Finds, please contact us at nexoyt2026gpt@gmail.com .

Updates

This policy will be updated before the integration becomes operational and whenever its data practices materially change.
